## 1. Which Canadians were arrested, charged, sentenced, released or subjected to exit bans abroad on national-security or espionage grounds in 2026, and what did Global Affairs Canada say?

### Takeaway
Two affairs missing from the register were verified: a dual Russian-Canadian professor, Nikolai Zyuzev, was sentenced in Russia to seven years for treason (announced 16 September 2026), with no Canadian reaction found; and Canadian men held for years without charge in north-east Syria were moved to Iraqi custody in January 2026 in a U.S. military operation, after which Ottawa asked for consular access, publicly contradicted Baghdad's claim that Canada would take them back (13 March) and declined in August to say whether repatriation was even discussed. No new espionage-type detention of a Canadian could be verified for China, Iran, India, Belarus, Venezuela, Algeria, Türkiye, the Gulf states or Central Asia.

Ziouzev"; Memorial identifies him as Nikolaï Fiedossievitch Ziouzev, a 70-year-old researcher and philosophy professor — [AFP via Noovo Info, 16 Sep 2026](https://www.noovo.info/nouvelles/international/article/un-russo-canadien-condamne-a-7-ans-de-prison-pour-haute-trahison-en-russie/)
- Russia, allegations (all FSB claims): from February 2022 he worked on a project against Russia's sovereignty and territorial integrity, planned to train abroad people who could form separatist movements among Indigenous peoples of Russia, distributed "extremist" documents, collected information for EU public bodies and think tanks, and received more than two million euros from 12 foreign organisations (German, French, British, Swedish) — [AFP via Noovo Info, 16 Sep 2026](https://www.noovo.info/nouvelles/international/article/un-russo-canadien-condamne-a-7-ans-de-prison-pour-haute-trahison-en-russie/)

### Inferences
- The Zyuzev case fits the post-2022 pattern of treason prosecutions of academics with foreign funding; the rapid sequence (custody in June, verdict in September), the upgrade from Article 275.1 to 275 and the unverifiable "full confession" are the points that make it look political. Whether Canada treats him as a consular case is unknown because Russia does not recognise dual nationality in practice and no Canadian statement was found.
- On Iraq, the two governments' incompatible readouts of the 10 March meeting, the later claim by the detainees' lawyer that Iraqi officials want Canada to take the men, and GAC's use of "operational security" for what it calls a consular file suggest that more is being discussed than Ottawa acknowledges. That is an inference, not a reported fact.
- The figures do not reconcile: nine Canadians known in Syrian detention, five in Iraqi custody per Baghdad, "at least five" per the lawyer, seven per the advocacy group.

### Gaps
- No Global Affairs Canada statement on Zyuzev, no confirmation of his Canadian residence or teaching post, and no information on an appeal or consular access were found.
- Charges, trial dates or sentences for Canadians in Iraq: none reported as of mid-November; nothing later than August was found in mainstream media.
- No verified 2026 arrest, exit ban or espionage charge involving a Canadian in China, Iran, India, Belarus, Algeria, Türkiye, the Gulf states or Central Asia; with only 16 searches, country-by-country queries for India, Algeria, Belarus, Central Asia and the Gulf could not be run.
- Schellenberg's retrial outcome remains unknown nine months after the death sentence was quashed.

## 2. Any 2026 sabotage, espionage, drone, cyber or disinformation incident targeting the Canadian-led brigade in Latvia (Operation REASSURANCE) or Canadian trainers and equipment linked to Ukraine (Operation UNIFIER)?

### Takeaway
No incident specifically aimed at Canadian soldiers was documented, but the picture is sharper than in the first pass: the Canadian commander of NATO's Multinational Brigade Latvia publicly ties a redeployment of Canadian troops toward Daugavpils to unspecified "subversion activities", says three drones were shot down in his first ten weeks, and in November listed espionage and arson among Russian hybrid tactics; Latvian airspace saw NATO shoot-downs on 8 June and 14 August and a string of drone alerts on 1, 3 and 10–11 November. Separately, the commander of Task Force Latvia was charged with a service offence over compensation and benefits and removed from command on 7 April (no foreign link alleged). Nothing was found on Operation UNIFIER.

### Cited Findings
- 14 August incident, Canadian Press version: residents of four areas including Daugavpils received a pre-dawn shelter alert; authorities later said a drone carrying explosives had entered from Belarus and was shot down by NATO fighter jets, with no injuries — [Canadian Press via CP24, 22 Aug 2026](https://www.cp24.com/news/canada/2026/08/22/latvia-drones-hybrid-warfare-and-the-redeployment-of-canadian-troops/)
- 14 August incident, Latvian military version: a NATO Baltic Air Policing fighter shot down a drone over Balvi municipality about half an hour after an airspace-threat alert issued after 4:00 a.m.; the Latvian military said it entered Latvian airspace after being affected by Russian electronic-warfare systems, during a mass drone attack on targets in Russia's Leningrad region; origin and payload not stated — [Militarnyi, 14 Aug 2026](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/nato-fighter-jet-shoots-down-drone-over-latvia/)
- Canadian commander's statements: Col. David Brassard, in command since 10 June, said "trois drones ont été abattus" since his arrival, called the situation "hybride", said attribution is difficult and that some drones are reportedly Ukrainian ones diverted by Russian jamming, and cited "activités de subversion" around Daugavpils without detail — [La Presse Canadienne via Noovo Info, 22 Aug 2026](https://www.noovo.info/nouvelles/international/article/lettonie-drones-guerre-hybride-et-deplacement-des-troupes-canadiennes/)
- Redeployment: the roughly 1,500–1,600 Canadian troops based at Ādaži since 2017 will increasingly be deployed, sometimes permanently, near the Russian and Belarusian borders; a first permanent platoon is in Daugavpils (about 80% Russian-speaking) and expansion continues until Christmas; NATO Maj.-Gen. Arnoud Stallmann said "we are actually at war" — [Canadian Press via CP24, 22 Aug 2026](https://www.cp24.com/news/canada/2026/08/22/latvia-drones-hybrid-warfare-and-the-redeployment-of-canadian-troops/)
- First NATO shoot-down: French Rafales on Baltic Air Policing shot down a drone over Latvia on 8 June 2026, which the Latvian army said had been diverted by Russian electronic warfare; a Ukrainian drone similarly diverted crashed into an oil facility in eastern Latvia on 7 May — [Kyiv Independent, 8 Jun 2026](https://kyivindependent.com/nato-jets-shoot-down-drone-over-latvia-in-1st-such-interception/)
- Political fallout in the host country: Latvia's defence minister resigned on 10 May after the Rēzekne incident and Prime Minister Siliņa resigned on 14 May — [Wikipedia: 2026 in Latvia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_in_Latvia)
- Change of command: Col. Kristopher Reeves handed the Multinational Brigade to Col. Brassard at Ādaži on 10 June 2026; the brigade has personnel from fourteen NATO nations — [National Defence, 10 Jun 2026](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/news/2026/06/canada-led-nato-multinational-brigade--latvia-conducts-transfer-of-command-authority.html)
- November, commander on hybrid threats: Brassard listed drone incursions and "espionage and even sabotage through acts of arson" as hybrid warfare he believes Russia is waging against several European countries including Latvia, with no specific case; he said going east is not "100 per cent safe"; about 3,000 soldiers from 14 nations and roughly 600 vehicles took part in exercise Resolute Reforger near Russia's border — [CTV News via CP24, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.cp24.com/news/canada/2026/11/06/a-mission-rehearsal-for-canadian-troops-training-near-the-border-of-russia/)
- November drone incidents: on 1 November a drone most likely of the Russian "Geran" type crossed Krāslava and Ludza and returned to Russia while a Latvian interceptor drone self-destructed; on 3 November an aircraft visually similar to Ukrainian drones probably crashed in Balvi district — [LSM, 3 Nov 2026](https://eng.lsm.lv/article/society/defence/03.11.2026-more-details-released-about-recent-drone-incidents-in-latvia.a670418/)
- Night of 10–11 November: three drones entered Latvian airspace, one flying from Lithuania to Estonia; NATO jets were scrambled, none was shot down; forces commander Kaspars Pudāns linked the alerts to a Ukrainian drone attack on port and oil facilities in the Leningrad region — [LSM, 11 Nov 2026](https://eng.lsm.lv/article/society/defence/11.11.2026-latvian-armed-forces-outline-reasons-for-multiple-drone-alerts.a671452/)
- Same night, regional view: at least four drones strayed into Baltic airspace; Lithuania's defence chief said some were diverted by electronic warfare; Moscow accuses Kyiv of using NATO airspace as cover — [Reuters via The Globe and Mail, 11 Nov 2026](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/world/article-nato-baltic-nations-drones-russia-ukraine-exchange-strikes/)
- Canadian counter-drone posture: the CAF is deploying anti-drone defences for personnel in Latvia; NATO's Adm. Pierre Vandier said a force not protected against drone swarms "is a force at risk" — [Global News, 30 Oct 2026](https://globalnews.ca/news/12127659/canadian-military-drone-technology-nato/)

- Ukraine support: the only 2026 DND item is a routine statement that the CAF and Belgian allies delivered equipment to Ukraine on the weekend before 8 June — [National Defence, 8 Jun 2026](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/news/2026/06/statement-from-the-honourable-david-mcguinty-minister-of-national-defence.html)

### Inferences
- The two accounts of 14 August differ on origin (Belarus and explosives per Canadian Press; Russian electronic-warfare diversion over Balvi, next to Russia, per the Latvian military). The likeliest reading of the year's incidents is spill-over from Ukrainian long-range strikes pushed off course by Russian jamming, but deliberate probing cannot be excluded for the drone of 1 November described as a "Geran" type.
- "Subversion" around Daugavpils and the November reference to espionage and arson are the first on-record statements by a Canadian commander linking the brigade's posture to hostile intelligence activity; they remain generic.
- The removal of the Task Force Latvia commander is a disciplinary matter with no stated security dimension; it is recorded with the lowest score.

### Gaps

- The CTV series part titled "Canadian soldiers face both 'insults' and 'open arms' from locals" could not be read beyond its headline — [CTV News, 4 Nov 2026](https://www.ctvnews.ca/canada/article/ctv-news-in-latvia-near-russias-border-canadian-soldiers-face-both-insults-and-open-arms-from-locals/)
