## 1. Any 2026 fire, explosion, vandalism or sabotage at Canadian critical infrastructure that police treated as suspicious or where a national-security unit became involved?

### Takeaway
Yes, but all of it is domestic and none drew a national-security unit: three fibre outages in three months blacked out north-west British Columbia (police and carriers blame copper thieves; an anonymous anarchist text hints otherwise without claiming anything), and a week-long string of about eleven arsons hit communications towers and pole lines in St. John's (one man charged, no motive given).
