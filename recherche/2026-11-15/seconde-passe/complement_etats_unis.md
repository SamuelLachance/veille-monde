## 1. After the 19 October 2026 Alberta referendum, did Elections Alberta, the Alberta government, CSIS, the RCMP, the SITE Task Force or the Foreign Influence Transparency Commissioner publish any assessment, investigation outcome or charges about foreign influence or the Centurion Project voter-list breach?

### Takeaway
No. As of 15 November 2026 I found no post-vote assessment of foreign influence from any of those bodies and no charge, penalty or investigation report in the Centurion Project breach; the three investigations (Elections Alberta, RCMP, privacy commissioner) were still described as open on 9 October.
