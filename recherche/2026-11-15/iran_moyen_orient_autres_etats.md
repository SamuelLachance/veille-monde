# Iran-, Middle East- and other-state-linked suspected covert activity connected to Canada in 2026

Research date: 2026-11-15. Everything below comes from pages opened in this session. Method note: the shared web-search budget for this run was exhausted after my first ~30 queries, so the second half of the work relied on directly opening URLs surfaced by those queries and on publisher listing pages (Global News author/tag pages, House of Commons evidence, canada.ca). Coverage of non-Iran states is therefore thin and is flagged in the Gaps sections. Companion structured file: `iran_moyen_orient_autres_etats.json` (35 events, 14 leads).

Naming note: HAYI = Harakat Ashab al-Yamin al-Islamiya = "Islamic Movement of the Companions of the Right" (IMCR) in Canadian intelligence documents. ITAC = Integrated Threat Assessment Centre (CIEM in French). KH = Kata'ib Hizballah.

## 1. Iran: plots, threats, proxies, arrests, and regime officials in Canada (incl. the Toronto shootings and the Masjoody case)

### Takeaway
A wave of shootings at Toronto-area synagogues (2 and 6–7 March) and at the U.S. consulate (10 March and 27 July) followed the 28 February start of the U.S./Israel–Iran war; Toronto police charged young "criminals for hire" recruited over encrypted apps, a U.S. complaint (15 May) attributes the consulate shooting and one synagogue attack to a Kata'ib Hizballah commander, and Canadian intelligence (ITAC) privately assessed Iran had "directed or enabled" criminal violence in Canada, but no Canadian authority has publicly attributed any specific attack to Iran. The Masood Masjoody killing, first feared to be transnational repression, led to first-degree murder charges against two fellow anti-regime diaspora activists with no state link alleged by police.

### Inferences
- The public record supports "paid criminal proxies, sponsor unproven" rather than "Iranian operation confirmed". The strongest Iran link is a U.S. complaint built on Al-Saadi's own recorded boasts; ITAC explicitly says the two Canadian shootings claimed by HAYI are not definitively linked.
- Timeline tension: the DOJ dates the HAYI campaign in Europe from about 9 March, yet the Toronto synagogue shootings occurred on 2 and 6–7 March; the complaint does not say which synagogue is meant.
- The same two handguns served Jewish, diplomatic and purely criminal targets, which fits a general-purpose gun-for-hire marketplace that a foreign sponsor could rent, as much as a dedicated Iranian network.
- Official messaging is inconsistent: a minister said "foreign entity" in the House; police say the payer is unknown; the PM declined comment; intelligence documents use probabilistic language; the RCMP commissioner said in March he saw no connection to any foreign entity in intimidation cases.
- A second consulate shooting on 27 July, after Al-Saadi's arrest, the June raids and an officer's death, suggests either other sponsors or copycat commissioning.
- Masjoody: the facts in the public record point to an intra-diaspora dispute; his own claim that the accused were regime-aligned is unproven and comes from a litigant courts had restrained.
- The recurring "intelligence-to-evidence" gap (CSIS disruptions without charges; CBSA withdrawing a case because evidence could not be used; sealed IRB files) is the common thread across the Iran files.

### Gaps
- No Canadian official attribution of any 2026 attack to Iran, and no terrorism charges against the Toronto shooters, were found. Whether INSET has laid or will lay national-security charges is unknown.
- I found no charge specifically tied to the 2 March Temple Emanu-El shooting.
- I could not read the U.S. complaint itself, the FBI affidavit on the Ghamari matter, or the paywalled Bureau articles; the Ghamari bounty details are unverified at source.
- Status of the Masjoody prosecution after the 25 March court date (preliminary inquiry, trial dates, cause of death) was not found.
- No 2026 update on the 2024 Irwin Cotler plot was found.

## 2. Canadians killed, detained or threatened by Iran in 2026 and Canada's response

### Takeaway
Ottawa confirmed one Canadian citizen died "at the hands of the Iranian authorities" during the January protest crackdown but released no identity or circumstances; Canada responded with statements, travel warnings, new sanctions (14 February) and wartime evacuations, and says it has no plan to restore diplomatic relations.

### Cited Findings
- 15 January: Foreign Minister Anita Anand said a Canadian citizen had "died in Iran at the hands of the Iranian authorities"; GAC confirmed awareness of the death but withheld details citing privacy; 3,054 Canadians/permanent residents were registered in Iran; Canadians advised to leave — [Global News, 15 Jan](https://globalnews.ca/news/11614452/canadian-citizen-dead-in-iran)
- Families in Ottawa and Toronto lost relatives on 8–9 January (e.g., Hossein Naseri, 73, father of an Ottawa resident, shot 9 January in Tehran); 9 January joint statement by Canada, Australia and the EU condemning killings of protesters — [Global News, 3 Feb](https://globalnews.ca/news/11647309/iran-protests-death-toll-mounts-regime-forces/)
- 14 February: Canada sanctioned seven individuals tied to the "Zindashti" transnational criminal network working with MOIS, an MOIS officer and a senior IRGC-QF Unit 840 official, citing among other things Iran's transnational repression; cumulative total stated as 478 Iranian individuals and entities — [Global Affairs Canada backgrounder, 14 Feb](https://www.canada.ca/en/global-affairs/news/2026/02/backgrounder-canada-imposes-additional-sanctions-against-iran.html)
- 2–4 February: advocates (Raoul Wallenberg Centre, Iranian Justice Collective) urged the RCMP to open a "structural investigation" to collect evidence on people in Canada linked to Iran's repression apparatus — [Iran International, 4 Feb](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202602040631)
- Since 28 February nearly 8,000 Canadians, permanent residents and family members had returned from the region and GAC had helped nearly 1,000 reach a safe third country (as of 12 March) — [CTV/CP24, 12 Mar](https://www.cp24.com/news/canada/2026/03/12/canada-has-cancelled-hundreds-of-visas-over-iran-connections/)
- Anand said Canada has no plans to restore diplomatic relations with Iran; Carney said having no embassies in Iran and Venezuela puts Canada "at a disadvantage" — [Global News, 3 Jul](https://globalnews.ca/news/11952212/iran-may-have-directed-recent-attacks-canada-intelligence-report/)
- Threats to people in Canada: a Canadian-resident journalist linked to Iran International was doxxed by Handala and received hundreds of violent threats (2025 incident, disclosed May 2026) — [CSIS Public Report 2025](https://www.canada.ca/en/security-intelligence-service/corporate/publications/csis-public-report-2025/operations-and-analysis.html)

### Inferences
- The gap between the minister's blunt attribution and the total absence of identifying detail is unusual; the most likely explanations are family wishes and protection of relatives in Iran.
- Canada's leverage is limited by the absence of an embassy (consular work runs through Ankara), which also limits its ability to verify detentions or deaths.

### Gaps
- Identity, date, place and circumstances of the Canadian's death were not found; no later official update was located.
- I found no confirmed 2026 case of a Canadian citizen newly detained in Iran, and no figures on Canadians killed or injured during the war itself. This may reflect the exhausted search budget rather than absence of cases.
- Whether the RCMP opened an Iran-focused structural investigation is unknown.

## 4. Israel-linked activity touching Canada

### Takeaway
The only 2026 development found is an advocacy-group report (CJPME, 13 May) compiling earlier media investigations into undisclosed Israeli government influence activity in Canada; the federal government gave no substantive response, and no 2026 official finding or new primary evidence was located.

### Cited Findings
- 13 May: CJPME report alleges covert Israeli influence, interference and transnational repression in Canada: an October 2023 poll by Aurora Strategies Global not disclosed as commissioned by the Israeli consulate; Israel's justice ministry quietly hiring a Toronto law firm in the settlement-wine labelling case; paid trips; a Diaspora Affairs Ministry-commissioned campaign of fake sites ("United Citizens for Canada") and hundreds of ChatGPT-powered accounts first exposed in 2024; alleged surveillance and doxxing of activists; it asks Ottawa to designate Israel a threat actor — [Middle East Eye, 13 May](https://www.middleeasteye.net/news/rogue-state-behaviour-israel-carrying-out-covert-influence-operations-canada-report-says)
- The firm in the labelling case is named as Torys; select Liberal MPs reportedly received the poll early; Global Affairs deferred to Public Safety, which sent a generic reply that the main perpetrators of foreign interference "remain unchanged"; NSICOP would not say whether Israel is among countries redacted from its 2024 report; mainstream Canadian media did not cover the report — [The Grind, 7 Jul](https://www.thegrindmag.ca/israel-engaged-in-illicit-foreign-interference-in-canada-report/)
- CSIS's 2025 public report names China, India, Russia, Iran and Pakistan as main perpetrators and says "these were not the only foreign states" — [CSIS Public Report 2025](https://www.canada.ca/en/security-intelligence-service/corporate/publications/csis-public-report-2025/operations-and-analysis.html)

### Inferences
- The best-documented element (the 2024 fake-account campaign) predates 2026; the 2026 report is a compilation by an interested party, and its transnational-repression claims are the least substantiated.
- Official silence can be read either as disagreement on the merits or as reluctance to name an ally; the public record does not settle which.

### Gaps
- No 2026 development on the RCMP "structural investigation" into the Israel–Hamas conflict was found (search budget ran out before this could be re-queried). No 2026 Citizen Lab finding of Israeli-made spyware used against people in Canada was found.
- The CJPME report itself was not opened; it is known here only through two sympathetic outlets.

## 6. Antisemitic or anti-Muslim attacks where foreign orchestration was suspected: what police and CSIS actually said

### Takeaway
Police and intelligence language stayed cautious all year: "criminals for hire", payer "to be determined", "realistic possibility", "yet to be definitively linked"; the only categorical foreign attribution by a Canadian official was one minister's remark in the House. Several high-profile attacks turned out to have domestic perpetrators with no foreign link reported.

### Cited Findings
- Toronto police: "bad actors are using criminal elements in our city"; "Who's paying for this? This is what we are trying to determine" — [JTA, 17 Jun](https://www.jta.org/2026/06/17/global/toronto-police-say-young-people-are-being-recruited-online-to-shoot-at-synagogues)
- Demkiw said in June police were keeping an open mind on the consulate link; Global News asked whether the synagogue and consulate shootings are connected to the Al-Saadi case and reported no answer — [Global News, 11 Aug](https://globalnews.ca/news/12016696/toronto-synagogue-shootings-3rd-arrest/)
- ITAC: Iran "directed or enabled" criminal violence in Canada (2 April), but two HAYI-claimed shootings "yet to be definitively linked" (29 June) — [Global News, 3 Jul](https://globalnews.ca/news/11952212/iran-may-have-directed-recent-attacks-canada-intelligence-report/); [Global News, 23 Jul](https://globalnews.ca/news/11974456/canadian-intelligence-report-threats-to-jewish-community/)
- CSIS: no violent extremist plots targeting the Muslim community observed in 2025, while multiple plots against Jewish people were foiled — [Global News, 1 May](https://globalnews.ca/news/11826598/csis-annual-report-violent-extremism-threats/)
- Gel-blaster attacks (30 April, 7 May): no evidence of links to other attacks or to the U.S. case — [CP24, 3 Jun](https://www.cp24.com/local/toronto/2026/06/03/more-arrests-made-in-targeted-and-organized-incidents-of-shots-being-fired-at-jewish-people/)
- Montreal, 22 June: a gunman killed SPVM Const. Mohamed Lamine Benredouane and bystander Michel (Michael) Mizrahi, an Israeli citizen, in Côte-des-Neiges; the shooter, Seth Scott Hatfield, 25, of Lethbridge, left a manifesto tied to incel ideology; UJA-Federation of Toronto said the Jewish community did not appear to be the target — [JTA, 23 Jun](https://www.jta.org/2026/06/23/global/israeli-citizen-michel-mizrahi-killed-in-montreal-shooting). An early report had described the victim as a rabbi and framed the attack as striking the heart of the Jewish community — [Algemeiner, 22 Jun](https://www.algemeiner.com/?p=774975). Later summaries describe the target as the Aylo (Pornhub) headquarters, Quebec's public security minister denying terrorism, and an RCMP INSET probe into ideologically motivated extremism — [Wikipedia, "2026 Montreal shooting"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Montreal_shooting) (tertiary source; primary Canadian Press page returned 403)
- Belleville, 20 September (Yom Kippur): Sean Ward, 29, a former CAF corporal released in 2022 after refusing COVID vaccination, fired on a police officer guarding Sons of Jacob Synagogue and died of his wounds; chief Murray Rodd called it "a targeted attack against the Jewish community"; sources describe antisemitic COVID conspiracy beliefs; no foreign link reported — [Global News, 22 Sep](https://globalnews.ca/news/12068908/belleville-gunman-covid-conspiracies-military/); [Global News, 24 Sep](https://globalnews.ca/news/12070914/soldier-synagogue-suing-1-million-vaccination/)
- Westmount (Montreal), June: fire at Temple Emanu-El-Beth Sholom apparently caused by a Molotov cocktail; a 38-year-old man arrested; no motive or foreign link stated — [Canadian Press via CP24, 21 Sep](https://www.cp24.com/news/canada/2026/09/22/after-belleville-ont-shooting-a-look-at-recent-violent-incidents-near-synagogues/)

### Inferences
- The Canadian pattern resembles the Australian and European ones in method (online recruitment, proof-of-attack videos, low lethality) but, unlike Australia, Canada has made no public state attribution.
- Early reporting on fast-moving attacks was unreliable (the Montreal "rabbi" framing); dashboard entries should be dated and re-checked against later findings.
- No anti-Muslim attack with suspected foreign orchestration surfaced in the material reviewed.

### Gaps
- No public statement by CSIS or the RCMP attributing a specific 2026 antisemitic attack to a foreign state was found.
- Details of the Westmount arson (date, charges, motive) and of any HAYI claim videos for Canadian attacks were not located.
- Source discrepancies to note: Temple Emanu-El dated 2 March (Jewish Insider) vs "March 3" (JTA); 27 vs 28 linked shootings; spellings Tracy-/Tracey-Stewart, Burgher/Burger, Pinizzotto/"Finizzotto" (Ynet); CBSA counts vary by cut-off date (26, 32, 37, 40).
